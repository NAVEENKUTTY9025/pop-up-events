Becoming a Vendor

We look for brands that offer high-quality, unique products with a strong aesthetic. Requirements include:

  • Original product designs or curated collections
  • Professional product photography
  • Previous market or retail experience (preferred but not mandatory)
  • Valid business license or tax ID
  • Liability insurance (can be arranged through our partners)

Our curatorial committee reviews each application individually.

Most applications are reviewed within 5-7 business days. You'll receive an email notification with our decision. During peak seasons, reviews may take up to 10 business days.

No, applying to become a Vendora vendor is completely free. You only pay when you secure a booth at one of our events. The application process has no hidden costs.

Events & Pricing

Booth pricing varies by event tier and booth size:

  • Boutique Vendor: $250/event (6x3 ft)
  • Premium Vendor: $450/event (8x4 ft)
  • Signature Vendor: $750/event (10x8 ft corner booth)

Prices exclude applicable taxes. Multi-event packages offer discounted rates.

Your booth fee includes: table and chairs (for most events), basic electricity access, Wi-Fi, event marketing inclusion, on-site vendor support, and post-event sales analytics. Premium and Signature tiers include additional benefits like featured social media posts and lookbook features.

We host 15-20 premium pop-up events annually across major cities. Events range from monthly rooftop markets to quarterly flagship festivals. Vendors receive early access to the event calendar via our portal.

Payments & Logistics

We process payouts within 3-5 business days after the event concludes. Funds are transferred directly to your linked bank account or preferred payment method. You can track all transactions in your vendor dashboard.

Yes! Our Logistics Concierge team offers:

  • Shipping coordination to and from event venues
  • Discounted rates with our carrier partners
  • On-site storage for multi-day events
  • Return shipping labels for unsold inventory (available for Signature vendors)

Cancellation policies vary by event. Generally:

  • 30+ days before event: Full refund minus 10% processing fee
  • 14-29 days before event: 50% refund
  • Less than 14 days: No refund, but credit toward a future event may be offered

Please review your event contract for specific terms.
