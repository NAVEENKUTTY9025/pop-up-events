Crafting Extraordinary Pop-Up Experiences

Vendora is the leading platform for luxury pop-up markets and event vendor management. Born in 2021, we've curated over 45 exclusive events across 12 cities, connecting premium artisans, designers, and brands with discerning audiences in high-footfall venues.

Our mission: to transform temporary retail into unforgettable brand moments. From rooftop soirées to cultural hub activations, we handle curation, logistics, and marketing — so vendors can focus on what they do best: creating.

Our Core Values

Celebrating authentic craftsmanship
  • Curatorial Excellence: Every vendor is hand-vetted by our luxury committee to ensure quality and uniqueness.
  • Elevated Experiences: We design atmospheres that convert — champagne bars, live music, and immersive branding.
  • Global Community: 400+ premium makers from Paris, Tokyo, NYC, and beyond connected through our platform.
  • Sustainable Luxury: We prioritize eco-conscious brands and zero-waste event practices.
  • Vendor Success: Transparent analytics, seamless payouts, and dedicated support for every seller.
