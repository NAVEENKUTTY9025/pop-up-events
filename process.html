The Vendora Method

Four streamlined stages from application to a successful event debut. Backed by dedicated support and premium resources.

1

Curatorial Application

Submit your brand story, product portfolio, and previous market experience. Our luxury committee reviews alignment with Vendora's aesthetic and quality standards within 5-7 business days.

2

Discovery & Contract

Upon acceptance, you'll receive a vendor onboarding kit, event calendar, and transparent fee structure. Sign digital agreements and select preferred markets.

3

Market Preparation

Access our vendor portal with setup guides, marketing toolkits, and logistics coordination. Schedule a styling consultation for your booth.

4

Live Selling & Analytics

Launch at the event with on-site support. Receive real-time sales dashboards and post-event insights to refine your strategy.

Typical Timeline

  • Application review: 5-7 days
  • Onboarding & training: 3-5 days
  • Event prep: 2-4 weeks before market
  • Post-event payout: 3-5 business days
